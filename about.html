Our Story

From a backyard obsession to Austin's premier hatchet lounge.

Born From The Blade

Founded in 2021 by a group of outdoor enthusiasts and competitive throwers, Ironwood Hatchet Lounge was built on a simple idea: that everyone deserves to feel the rush of a perfect throw.

We've spent thousands of hours perfecting our lanes, training our coaches, and curating an atmosphere that feels like a high-energy mountain lodge in the heart of the city.

Our Mission

"To build a community where everyone—from the first-timer to the seasoned pro—can experience the thrill of the throw in a safe, celebratory, and unforgettable environment."

Expansion Milestones

A look back at how we got here.

2021: The Idea Conceived

The founders first drew the blueprint for Ironwood on the back of a napkin after a tournament in Oregon.

2022: Venue Built & Launched

We opened our doors in downtown Austin with 12 lanes and a mission to change the local entertainment scene.

2023: First League Launched

Our inaugural WATL-sanctioned league season sold out in under 48 hours.

2024: Tournament Program Started

Launched our monthly "Ironwood Open" series, attracting top throwers from across the state.
