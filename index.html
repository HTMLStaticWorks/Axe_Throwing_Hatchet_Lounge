Got Questions?

Everything you need to know before you throw.

Absolutely not! About 90% of our first-time guests have never held an axe. Our certified coaches will spend the first 10-15 minutes of your session teaching you the safety rules and the perfect throwing technique.

Participants must be 12 years or older to throw. Anyone under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Evening sessions after 8 PM are 21+ only.

Walk-ins are welcome during all open hours, but lanes fill up fast—especially on weekends. We highly recommend booking online in advance to guarantee your spot.

Closed-toe shoes are mandatory—no exceptions. We recommend comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows for a full range of arm motion.

Yes, we have a full bar featuring local craft beers, wine, and themed cocktails. However, we have a strict policy: safety first. Any guest showing signs of intoxication will not be permitted to throw.

Our standard lanes accommodate up to 6 throwers. For larger groups, we book adjacent lanes so your party can stay together.
