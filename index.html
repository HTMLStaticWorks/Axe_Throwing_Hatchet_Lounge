Safety First, Bullseyes Second

Axe throwing is extremely fun and, thanks to our strict rules, completely safe. Check out our core safety guidelines.

Closed-Toe Shoes

Absolutely mandatory for all throwers in the lane area. We recommend sneakers or comfortable boots for stability.

One Thrower in Lane

Only one person is permitted inside the throwing box at a time. All other guests must remain behind the safety line.

Safe Hand Transfer

Never toss or hand an axe to someone directly. Always place it back on the designated target holder before the next person retrieves it.

Follow the Coach

Our certified lane coaches monitor all throws. Their commands are final and must be respected to maintain a safe environment.
