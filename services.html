Safety First

Your safety is our top priority. We've designed our venue with state-of-the-art safety features and strict protocols to ensure a worry-free experience.

  • Anti-bounce border technology in every lane.
  • Mandatory safety briefings for all first-timers.
  • Certified axe coaches monitoring every session.

Pro Tip

Closed-toe shoes are strictly required for all throwers. We recommend comfortable, athletic footwear for the best experience.
